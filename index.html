How it works

Notarized actions, not intentions.

1 · Witness (architecture)

The XORAS substrate re-executes agent work against two implementations. The v0.1.0 download ships the receipt chain only — verification primitives for your own harness.

2 · Notary

A signed receipt is written to a WORM chain. Each receipt commits to the previous one — editing history breaks verification of everything after it.

3 · Prove

Any third party can re-verify the chain offline with the same key. No cloud, no black box, no trust in the agent required.
