Security model & honest scope

What the controls are — and what they are not.

The download artifact

xoras-notary v0.1.0 is a single Python file, standard library only. No network calls, no telemetry, no dependencies. It issues and verifies HMAC-SHA256-signed receipts chained WORM-style: each receipt commits to the hash of the previous one, so altering any earlier receipt breaks verification of all later ones.

Scope, stated plainly. v0.1 uses symmetric keys: the verifier needs the same key that signed. It proves that a payload was notarized by the key holder at a recorded time and that the chain is intact. It does not judge whether the agent's action was semantically correct. Public-key attestation (verify without sharing the signing key) is the v1 roadmap item.

The verification architecture

  • Dual-substrate witnesses. A native Zig verifier and an independent Python guard recompute the same Merkle bytes. They must agree, or the artifact does not advance.
  • WORM lineage. State history is append-only; there is no in-place mutation path for recorded nodes.
  • Sandboxed candidate validation. Candidate code is executed inside an OS-enforced sandbox (Apple sandbox-exec on macOS — network and writes outside allowed paths denied; bubblewrap on Linux), not under a bare subprocess with the host's privileges.
  • Formal proofs. Committed SMT-LIB v2 proofs are checked with Z3; the committed invariants discharge UNSAT.
  • Theatrical tests are quarantined, not shipped. A fabricated test that printed hard-coded PASS lines was removed from the certified tree and archived with a receipt. This page is the statement that we do not ship evidence we did not produce.

What we do not claim

  • We are not a certification body and issue no compliance certificates. Artifacts we produce are evidence, not legal opinions.
  • We make no claim that a sandboxed candidate is safe in general — only that the specific sandbox checks (no network, no writes to protected paths, timeouts, exit codes) were exercised and passed at verification time.
  • No third-party auditor has reviewed this page or the download as of its publication date.

Reporting a vulnerability

Email hello@xoras.info with the subject line SECURITY: xoras-notary. Include reproduction steps and what you observed. Do not open public issues for unpatched reports until coordinated disclosure. We acknowledge receipt and will not retaliate against good-faith research.
